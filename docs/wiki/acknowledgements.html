Acknowledgements

locationjoystick is built on open-source projects. Thanks to everyone who works on them. For what the app sends to online services, see Settings → External services.

Maps

Everything behind the map, routes and place search.

ProjectUsed forLicense
MapLibre Android SDK Draws the interactive map, including the floating map overlay. BSD 2-Clause
OpenStreetMap Supplies the default map you see on every map screen. ODbL (© OpenStreetMap contributors)
Amap (高德地图) An optional map source you can choose in Settings. © AutoNavi · GCJ-02 coordinate system
OSRM (Open Source Routing Machine) Makes routes follow real roads, for guided routes and roaming. BSD 2-Clause
Nominatim (OpenStreetMap Foundation) Finds places by name in the route creator and the favorites map picker. Data: ODbL · Software: GPL-2.0
Photon (Komoot) Finds places by name when the main search cannot be reached. Data: ODbL (OpenStreetMap) · Software: Apache-2.0

Language

The tools the app is written in.

ProjectUsed forLicense
Kotlin (JetBrains) The language the whole app is written in. Apache 2.0
Kotlin Coroutines (JetBrains) Keeps location updates running smoothly in the background. Apache 2.0
Kotlin Serialization (JetBrains) Saves and loads your data for backup and transfer. Apache 2.0
Hilt (Dagger) (Google) Keeps the app stable by connecting its parts reliably. Apache 2.0

Screens

What you see and how you move around.

ProjectUsed forLicense
Jetpack Compose (Google) Builds every screen and button in the app. Apache 2.0
Material 3 (Google) Gives the app its look: buttons, cards, dialogs and colors. Apache 2.0
Google Material Symbols The show/hide joystick icon on the floating widget. Apache 2.0
AndroidX Navigation Compose (Google) Moves you between screens and opens the side menu. Apache 2.0
AndroidX Lifecycle (Google) Keeps the app running smoothly when you rotate the screen or switch apps. Apache 2.0

Storage

Your data stays on your phone.

ProjectUsed forLicense
AndroidX Room (Google) Stores your routes and favorites on your phone. Apache 2.0
AndroidX DataStore (Google) Remembers your settings. Apache 2.0

Network

Sends the few requests the app makes.

ProjectUsed forLicense
Retrofit (Square) Sends requests for place search and road-following routes. Apache 2.0
OkHttp (Square) Carries every request the app sends over the internet. Apache 2.0

QR codes

Used for transferring your data between phones.

ProjectUsed forLicense
ZXing (Zebra Crossing) (Google) Creates and reads the QR codes for phone-to-phone transfer. Apache 2.0
AndroidX CameraX (Google) Lets the QR scanner use your camera when you receive a transfer. Apache 2.0
