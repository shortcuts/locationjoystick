Backup & Transfer

Save your routes, favorites, and settings to keep them safe or move them to another phone.

Every action is in the More actions menu at the top right of Settings. Steps below name the section and item to tap.

What is included

  • Routes, with each route's type, stops, waits, and speed profile.
  • Favorites, with their categories and your Home favorite.
  • Speed profiles.
  • Map button and widget setup, including their display order.
  • Sort choices for your routes and favorites lists.
  • Location randomness settings and roaming defaults.

Captured location lists are not included. Permissions are not included either.

Back up to a file

  1. Open Settings and open the menu. Under Export, tap Export settings.
  2. Your phone's share menu opens.
  3. Pick where the backup file goes, such as Files, a drive, or a chat.

Save the file somewhere you can find it later.

Restore from a file

  1. Open the menu. Under Import, tap Import from file.
  2. Choose your backup file.
  3. Choose Add to my data or Replace all data.

Add to my data

Keeps your routes and favorites. The backup's items are added next to them. An item you already have is overwritten, so near-duplicates can appear.

Replace all data

Deletes your routes and favorites first. You end up with only the backup's.

Both choices apply the backup's settings and setup over yours.

Transfer to another phone

Copy your backup straight to another phone with a QR code or short code. Both phones must be on the same Wi-Fi network.

Send with a QR code

  1. Open the menu. Under Export, tap Export via QR code.
  2. A dialog shows a QR code and a short backup code.
  3. Let the other phone scan the QR code. Or read out or message the short code.

Receive on the other phone

  1. Open the menu. Under Import, tap Import from QR code or Import via code.
  2. Scan the QR code or type the short code. Phone hotspots can block typed codes, so scan if typing fails.
  3. Choose Add to my data or Replace all data, as in Restore from a file.

Data goes directly between the two phones. See the privacy policy.

Import from other apps

GPX

A common file for GPS tracks and routes. Each track or route becomes its own straight-line route. Named places become favorites.

GPX limits

Routes with too many points are skipped. A message after the import says how many.

GPS Joystick

Routes and favorites from a GPS Joystick export.

GPS Joystick backups

Backup files from that app do not work. Export GPX there instead.

YAMLA

Favorites, plus walk, run, and bike speeds. Routes in a YAMLA file are not imported.

Move to a new phone or after a factory reset

  1. On the old phone, do Back up to a file. Keep the file off the phone, such as in a drive.
  2. On the new phone, install the app and finish the first-run setup. Permissions are not in the backup.
  3. Under Import, tap Import from file. Choose the backup, then Replace all data.

Both phones on the same Wi-Fi? Transfer with a QR code instead.

Switch from the Play Store version

To move between the Play Store app and the GitHub APK, carry your data over with a file.

  1. Back up to a file and keep it off the phone.
  2. Uninstall the old version, then install the new one.
  3. Under Import, tap Import from file and choose your backup.

Reset all data

Permanently delete all your favorites, routes, and settings.

  1. Open the menu. Under Danger, tap Reset all data.
  2. Confirm the action. It cannot be undone.

Permissions stay on your phone, so you don't need to repeat setup.
