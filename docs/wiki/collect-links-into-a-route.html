Collect map links into a route

Turn map links from other apps into one saved route.

Before you start

  • Capture setup is done. See Set up Capture.
  • A source of map links, such as a chat or a list of places.

Steps

  1. Open Capture from the Home screen or side menu.
  2. Turn on Capture mode and choose List.
  3. Tap each map link in the other app. A short message confirms every point.
  4. Return to Capture. Your points show in capture order.
  5. Type a route name and tap Save as route.

Result

The route appears in your Routes list. You need at least two points.

More

The app reorders points so each stop is near the last. Choose Keep original to keep your order. More options are in Capture.
