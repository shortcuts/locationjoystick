Hiding the fake location

Android marks every location from a fake GPS app as fake. Any app on the phone can read that mark. No fake GPS app can remove it by itself.

Apps that hide the mark change the app that checks it. For example, they install a modified copy of that app. Google Play does not allow apps that modify other apps. So locationjoystick does not hide the mark.

locationjoystick makes the signal look real in other ways. See GPS realism in Settings.
