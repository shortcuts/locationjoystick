Draw a line

Draw a line on the map with your finger. Then walk along it or save it as a route. The line is followed exactly as you draw it. It does not snap to roads.

Turn it on

The Draw line button is off by default. Turn it on in Settings under Menus. It then shows on the map and on the floating map.

Draw and walk

Prerequisites: Draw line is turned on.

  1. Tap the Draw line button. It lights up.
  2. Drag your finger across the map.
  3. Lift your finger. A sheet opens with your points.
  4. Tap Start route to walk the line. Tap Save route to keep it.

While Draw line is on, dragging draws instead of moving the map. Tap the button again to move the map.

Tips

  • Zoom in first for a precise line.
  • A very short drag draws nothing.
  • Drawing ends after each line. Tap the button to draw another.
