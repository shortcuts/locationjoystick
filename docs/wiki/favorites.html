Favorites

A favorite is a named place you walk or teleport to. Favorites stay on your device. See the privacy policy. Move favorites to another phone with Backup & Transfer.

The Favorites list

  • Tap a favorite to walk there (straight line or via roads) or teleport there. Teleport is hidden when Hide teleport features is on.
  • Sort favorites A–Z, Z–A, newest, or oldest. The widget's favorites lists use the same order.
  • Tap the house icon on a favorite to make it your Home. The app then starts there instead of your last location. Only one favorite can be Home. Tap the house again to clear it.
  • The three-dot menu lets you edit a favorite, copy its coordinates, send them as a message, or delete it.
  • The share button next to Sort sends your current location as decimal degrees. The widget has the same button.

Search and areas

  • The search box finds favorites by name or area as you type.
  • Favorites with an area are grouped under the area name. Favorites without an area are listed at the bottom.
  • The map sheet and widget have the same search. Closing the list clears it.

How to add a favorite

Tap the add button at the bottom right of the Favorites screen. Pick one of these methods.

  • From coordinates: type latitude and longitude in separate boxes.
  • Paste coordinates: paste a pair in decimal form or degrees, minutes and seconds.
  • From map: pick a point on the map.
  • Use current location: save where you are now.

The paste box on the map can also save a pasted pair as a favorite.

From map

"From map" opens a full-screen map. Tap to place a pin, or search for a place by name. The search uses an external service.

  • Search by place name to move the map to an address or landmark.
  • Tap the map to move the pin to an exact spot.
  • Confirm to save the pin with a name.

Hot locations

In Settings, turn on Show hot locations to add popular places around the world to your favorites. They include cities, parks, and landmarks. This is off by default.

  • Turning it off removes only the entries this feature added. Your own favorites stay.
  • Each hot location uses its country as its area name.
  • The list updates online. Places that are removed disappear from your favorites while this is on.
