Language

locationjoystick's interface is available in English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese.

Changing the language

On Android 13 and newer, go to your phone's Settings → Apps → locationjoystick → Language, and pick the language you want. The app switches immediately.

On Android 9 through 12, your phone doesn't offer a per-app language setting, so locationjoystick provides its own: open the app, and during setup (or any time afterward in Settings → Menus → Appearance) pick a language from the "Language" row. The app restarts itself to apply the choice immediately.
