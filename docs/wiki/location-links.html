Location links

A location link is a map pin sent from another app or another person. Opening one shows the spot on the map and asks what to do. To collect many links into a route instead, see Capture.

Share a location

On the map, press and hold a point, then tap Share this location. The link opens this app on that spot.

Open a link

What happens when you open a link

  1. The app opens, or comes to the front if already running.
  2. The map moves to the linked location.
  3. A popup asks what to do.

Teleport here

Moves your fake position there at once.

Walk here

Walks there at your current speed.

Walk here via roads

Follows real roads.

Do nothing

Closes the popup. Your position does not change.

Opening links from other apps

Other apps do not need this app's share button. Pick this app from the share list of any app that shares a location. The same popup appears.

Map links from many apps work, including the Google Maps Share button. The app follows shortened links to find the coordinates. If a link cannot be read, a short message appears at the bottom.

A shared link did nothing? See Troubleshooting.

Build a link by hand (for developers)

You can build a link by hand for scripts or automation. The Share this location button produces the https:// form below. 

https://locationjoystick.shrtcts.fr/?lat=LAT&lon=LON

lat

Type: decimal

Valid range: −90 to 90

Latitude

lon

Type: decimal

Valid range: −180 to 180

Longitude

Example:

https://locationjoystick.shrtcts.fr/?lat=35.6762&lon=139.6503
  • Both lat and lon are required. The app ignores a link lacking either one.
  • The app ignores a link with coordinates outside the valid range.
  • No key or sign-in is needed.
  • A second format also works: locationjoystick://open?lat=LAT&lon=LON. Use the https:// link when sharing with other people.
