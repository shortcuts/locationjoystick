Loop a route

Walk a saved route back and forth, as many times as you like.

Before you start

  • A saved route with at least two stops. See how to add a route.

Steps

  1. Tap the routes button on the map.
  2. Tap your route. A preview of its path appears.
  3. Check that Loop is on. It is on when the sheet opens.
  4. Turn on Return to start to walk back to the first stop after the last one.
  5. Tap Start.

Result

You walk to the first stop. At the last stop, you turn around and walk back to the first stop. Then you walk forward again, until you stop.

More

To stop, open the route controls on the map or the widget and tap Stop. See all replay options.
