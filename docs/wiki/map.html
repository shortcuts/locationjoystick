Map

The map shows your location as a marker. Buttons on it open search, paste, routes, favorites, and roaming. Map tiles come from Settings → Menus → Map source. If the map does not appear, see Troubleshooting.

What you see

You open the app with spoofing on

The map loads. With spoofing off, the Home screen loads first. Open the side menu to reach the map.

You never set a position

The map starts at your real location.

You drag the map

It stops following your marker. Tap the center button to follow again. With spoofing off, it centers on your real location and keeps your saved position.

A walk, route, or roam is idle

Its button is grey.

A walk, route, or roam is moving

Its button is green.

A walk, route, or roam is paused

Its button is orange. Tap it to open the controls, then tap Resume.

To stop or pause a walk, tap its button on the map and use the controls. To change speed, use the speed button on the widget.

Search for a place

  1. Tap the search button (magnifying glass).
  2. Type a place name, address, or landmark.
  3. Pick a result to place a marker there.
  4. Long-press the marker to walk or teleport.
  • Recent searches appear before you type. They stay on your device.
  • Searching does not move you. You choose to walk or teleport from the marker.
  • Have exact coordinates? Use Paste coordinates.
  • For privacy details, see Settings → External services.

Paste coordinates

Prerequisites: Turn on the paste button in Settings → Menus → App Features.

  1. Tap the paste button (clipboard icon) on the map or widget.
  2. Paste or type coordinates, one point per line. Formats are listed in Routes.
  3. Choose an action.
  • One point: Teleport, Walk, Walk via roads, or Save favorite. Teleport works offline.
  • Two or more points: Save route, Start route, and the route options in Routes.
  • Start route plays the points without saving them. The next paste replaces it.
  • Save route never overwrites an existing route, even with the same name.
  • The app ignores lines it does not recognize. If none are valid, it says so.
  • The box grows to 10 lines, then scrolls. Select all and Clear all sit below it.

Open a GPX file

Open a GPX file from Files, Downloads, Share, or a chat app. The paste sheet opens titled "Open GPX" with the points filled in. Pick an action as above. Save route suggests the file name. If the file is not valid GPX, a message says so.

Tap to move

Long-press a point on the map, then pick an action from the sheet.

Teleport here

Jump to the point at once. Any running or paused route or roam ends first. Works offline. If spoofing is paused, tap Start first.

Walk here

Walk to the point in a straight line at your current speed.

Walk here via roads

Walk along real roads at your current speed. See when roads cannot load.

Add next point

While walking, add a stop. The first tap chains your start, destination, and new point. Later taps add more stops. If you are following roads, the new stretch does too.

Share this location

Open the share menu with a link to the point. See Location Links.

While a saved route plays

Choose Stop route and teleport, Stop route and walk here, or Finish route and walk here. Finish adds the point to the route's end.

Walking via roads, road-following routes, and roaming load paths from an online service. If it is unreachable, the app uses a straight line for that stretch.

Teleport wait

Some games block quick jumps. Before you teleport, the app shows a suggested wait based on the distance jumped. The wait is advice only. Nothing is blocked.

  • The wait shows on the map's tap sheet, favorites lists, the floating widget, and the Group Sync follower screen.
  • It reads "No wait needed" when the wait is over or you have not teleported yet.
  • The clock starts at each teleport, from any screen. Walking never affects it.
  • Under 10 m: None
  • 10 m: 3 seconds
  • 100 m: 15 seconds
  • 500 m: 30 seconds
  • 1 km: 2 minutes
  • 5 km: 6 minutes
  • 10 km: 11 minutes
  • 25 km: 14 minutes
  • 30 km: 22 minutes
  • 65 km: 25 minutes
  • 81 km: 35 minutes
  • 100 km: 45 minutes
  • 250 km: 1 hour
  • 500 km: 1 hour 15 minutes
  • 750 km: 1 hour 30 minutes
  • 1,000 km or more: 2 hours

Between rows, the lower row applies. Distance is a straight line from your position to the target.

Routes sheet

  1. Tap the routes button on the map.
  2. Tap a route to preview its path.
  3. Choose Start, Loop, or Reverse.
  4. Use Previous stop and Next stop to skip waypoints. A count like 3/12 shows your current stop.

See Replay options for more.

Favorites sheet

  1. Tap the favorites button on the map.
  2. Tap a favorite and pick Set location (teleport), Walk to location (straight line), or Walk via roads.
  3. Type in the search box to filter by name or area. Closing the sheet clears it.
  4. Tap Save current location to add where you are now.

Each favorite shows its straight-line distance. Road distance is longer. Teleport ends any running or paused route or roam.

Roaming sheet

Prerequisites: Pause or stop a playing route first. Only one roaming mode runs at a time.

  1. Tap the roaming button on the map.
  2. Choose a mode.
  3. Set radius, distance, speed, or loop count. Defaults are in Settings → Roaming.
  4. Tap Generate to preview the path.
  5. Tap Start roaming. It keeps going with the screen off.

Walk around the block

Walks to random spots within a radius.

Planting

Walks a spiral that opens out, then tightens. One spiral is one loop.

Changing speed during roaming replaces the speed you set here.
