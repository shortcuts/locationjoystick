Overlays

Overlays let you control your fake location from on top of your game. If one does not appear, see Troubleshooting.

Overlays need the Display over other apps permission. First-run setup walks you through it. On some phones, such as Xiaomi or Oppo, it can reset after a restart. If an overlay disappears, check your phone's autostart and battery settings.

Floating joystick

The floating joystick is a round control that stays on screen. Drag it to move your fake location. Show or hide it from the floating widget. Spoofing stays on.

  • Drag the center dial to move. Pull farther out to move faster, up to your selected speed.
  • Let go to stop. The stick returns to center and your position stays put.
  • Drag the outer ring to move the joystick anywhere on screen.
  • Moving the stick pauses any walk, route or roam so you can steer. See the Lock joystick control below.

Floating widget

The floating widget is a small panel of quick controls. Tap the round button to expand it. It stays on screen when you switch apps. Buttons are grey when idle, green while their activity is moving and orange while it is paused.

  • Tap the round button again to collapse the panel. Drag it to move the widget.
  • Press and hold the app icon to show Pause and Stop.
  • Pause turns spoofing off and keeps the widget. Other buttons fade until spoofing is on again, then Pause becomes Start.
  • Stop ends spoofing and removes the widget. To only hide the widget button, use Hide floating widget in Settings.
  • Choose which controls appear, and their order, in Settings → Menus → App Features.
  • Lists and route options follow Light or Dark mode from Settings → Menus → Appearance.
  • The route start sheet has the same options as Replay options.

Available widget controls

Map shortcut (map icon)

Opens a small map above your game. Taps outside the map go to the game.

  • Use the expand button for full screen, and shrink it again without losing your place.
  • Walk, teleport, paste coordinates or start roaming from it.
  • The N arrow keeps north at the top. Turn it off to rotate the map.

Default: On

Show / hide joystick (joystick icon)

Shows or hides the joystick. Moving the stick pauses the running activity so you can steer.

Default: On

Lock joystick

Shows the joystick if hidden, then keeps its direction after you let go.

  • Drag to the center to stop while keeping lock on. Pull outward to move again.
  • Tap the button again to unlock.
  • Locking alone does not pause an activity. Moving the stick does.

Default: On

Pause and resume

Moving the joystick pauses walk, route, roam or planting. Destination and progress are kept.

  • Tap Resume to continue, or choose a new destination to start over.
  • If you follow a leader, following turns off and you stay in the group.
  • Touching the very center, or dragging the outer ring, pauses nothing.

Default: Automatic

Routes picker

Lists your routes. Tap one to start, rename, share or delete it. While a route runs, it shows pause, stop, Previous / Next stop and your stop count, such as 3/12. Search and sort work as on the Routes screen.

Default: On

Favorites picker

Lists your favorites with teleport and walk shortcuts. Open one to rename or delete it. Save your current position, or share it. Search and sort work as on the Favorites screen.

Default: On

Paste coordinates

Opens a small box at the bottom of the screen to paste coordinates. You can still use the app above it.

  • Shrink the box to a bar to copy more points in another app.
  • Press and hold the button for a Capture shortcut.
  • If you paused spoofing, tap Start before you paste.

See Map → Paste coordinates for formats.

Default: Off. Turn it on in Settings → Menus → App Features.

Roaming

Opens Walk around the block and Planting, so you can roam without opening the app. While roaming runs, tap it for pause and stop.

Default: On

Altitude override

Type a fixed height for your fake location. It replaces the automatic height until you reset it in Settings → Movement & GPS.

Default: Off. Turn on Show altitude override button in Settings → Menus → Privacy.

Tap to Walk crosshair

Places a clear layer over your app. Your next tap becomes your walk destination. See Tap to Walk.

Default: Shown only when Tap to Walk is on

Speed cycle

One tap switches to the next speed. The icon shows the current speed. It applies at once to the joystick, walking, roaming and a playing route.

Default: On

Tap to Walk

Tap to Walk gives you two shortcuts to move your fake location with one tap. Both are off by default. Floating map quick walk is under Settings → Menus → Privacy. The screen overlay is under Settings → Menus → Tap to Walk.

Settings

Scale

Meters per pixel on your screen. Range 0.01 to 1.0. Raise it for a zoomed-out game map and lower it for a zoomed-in one.

Default: 0.23

Compass orientation

Keeps taps accurate when the game map rotates. Without it, taps assume north is at the top. Uses an Android accessibility feature.

Default: Off

Game app

Pick your game from the list, then tap Test to check detection on your phone.

Default: Select app

Floating map quick walk

Turn it on in Settings → Menus → Privacy. A tap on the floating map then walks to that spot at once. It skips the popup that asks whether to walk, teleport or use roads.

Screen overlay

The overlay adds a crosshair button to the floating widget. Tap it once to place a clear layer over your current app. Your next tap anywhere becomes your walk destination, and the layer disappears. To cancel, tap the close button in the bottom-right corner.

Risks

  • Some apps can detect a fake location and act on your account. Use this feature only in apps where you accept that risk.
  • Some apps can notice the compass helper. If your game limits features, turn off the locationjoystick compass entry in your phone's accessibility settings.
  • Turning the overlay on opens a confirmation screen first.
  • Nothing turns on until you tap Agree.

Compass orientation

When the overlay opens, the app takes a picture of the screen and looks for your game's compass icon in the top-right corner. It uses the compass direction to rotate the walk target, so it lands correctly on a turned map. It runs once you turn on the accessibility feature below.

Setup

Before Accessibility settings open, a full-screen message explains what the app looks at and why. The picture of your screen never leaves your phone. Tap Agree to continue, or Not now to keep it off. You see this message:

  • during first-run setup, in the optional Compass orientation step
  • the first time you turn Tap to Walk on
  • the first time you tap the crosshair button
  1. Tap Agree on the message.
  2. Find locationjoystick compass in the list and turn it on.
  3. Return to locationjoystick. Compass detection is now on.

To see the message again, go to Settings → Menus → Tap to Walk → Compass orientation and tap Open Settings.

Check that it works

  1. Pick your game from the Game app list. Without a pick, switch to your game, switch back, then continue.
  2. Tap Test. The app briefly switches to your game, reads its compass, and returns.
  3. The app shows the detected direction. If it finds nothing, make sure the compass is visible in the game.
