Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-10

App data

The app keeps all your data on your device: routes, favorites, speeds, and settings. There is no account and no cloud. The app never sends this data to a server. Group Sync and QR transfer send data directly from one device to another over your local network. See Group Sync.

Internet use

Some features load information from public services: map images, road routes, place search, and elevation. The app sends only what each request needs, such as the map area or the text you search for. It sends nothing that identifies you. The full list of services is in Settings.

Location permission

The app uses your real location only to center the map and to record routes. It never uploads or shares your location.

Update check

About once a day, when you open the app, it asks GitHub if a newer release exists. It uses the answer for the "update available" badge on the Home screen. The request does not include your routes, favorites, settings, or location. You cannot turn this check off. It does nothing when you are offline.

Camera permission

The app uses the camera only to scan QR codes when you transfer data between devices. It does not store or send photos or video.

Compass orientation

"Compass orientation" is an optional feature (Settings → Menus → Tap to Walk). It is off by default. When you turn it on, the app takes a picture of your screen just before you tap a location. It reads the compass direction in the app you are using. This keeps your tap accurate when the map does not point north.

The app reads the picture on your device. It never saves or sends the picture. This feature is not an accessibility tool for people with disabilities.

This website

This policy covers the app. The website you are reading is separate. Its sidebar shows the GitHub star count, which it loads from GitHub. Its search box sends what you type to Algolia, the search provider.

Contact

Questions or requests? On GitHub, you can report a bug or request a feature.
