Reconnect accessory

Let the app tap the connect button for you when your Bluetooth accessory drops.

Before you start

  • Your game shows the accessory button in the top-right corner of the screen.
  • Your phone runs Android 11 or newer.

Steps

  1. Open Settings → Menus → Reconnect accessory.
  2. Turn on Reconnect accessory automatically.
  3. Read the notice and tap Accept.
  4. Turn on the app in the Android Accessibility screen that opens.

Whether the screen helper is on is shown in the Screen access block above this section. If the helper is off, this section tells you and offers an Open Settings button.

Result

About every minute, the app looks at your screen. It taps the connect button only if the accessory shows as disconnected.

If the accessory fails to reconnect several times in a row, the app sends you a notification. Tap it to open the app.

Limits and risks

  • The app never taps when the button is hidden or looks connected.
  • The picture of your screen stays on your phone and is deleted at once.
  • Turn the switch off at any time to stop the checks.
  • Some games act on accounts that use automation. Use this only where you accept that risk.
