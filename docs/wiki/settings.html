Movement & GPS

Speed profiles

Edits apply at once, even during movement. The km/h ↔ mph switch changes every speed in the app. There is no upper limit. Speeds below 0.04 km/h are raised to 0.04 km/h, because zero blocks movement.

ProfileDefaultWhat it does
Slow Walk 1.1 km/h Strolling. Off in the Speed cycle.
Walk 2.0 km/h Regular walking pace.
Run 8.0 km/h Jogging. Follows footpaths when Follow roads is on.
Bike 15.0 km/h Cycling. Follows bike lanes when Follow roads is on.
Drive 54.0 km/h Driving. Off in the Speed cycle. Above the anti-cheat limit below.

Walk, Run, and Bike are in the Speed cycle by default. Slow Walk and Drive are not.

Anti-cheat warning

Speeds above 28.8 km/h (17.9 mph) show this message: "Speed exceeds 8 m/s, may trigger anti-cheat in some games". It is a notice only and changes nothing.

Location randomness

Small random changes to each location update, like real GPS drift. Set a value to 0 to turn it off. Wobble and altitude show in feet when you use mph.

OptionDefaultWhat it does
Wobble when still 0.8 m Largest random shift while you stand still. Maximum 50 m.
Wobble while moving 0.3 m Largest random shift while you move. Keep it below the still value. Maximum 50 m.
Max step per tick 1 m How far the shift can change in one update. Allowed range: 0.1 to 5 m.
Speed wobble when still 5% Random percentage added to or taken from the reported speed at rest. 0 looks perfectly still.
Speed wobble while moving 5% Random percentage added to or taken from the reported speed while you move. Allowed range: 0 to 50%.
Idle wobble frequency 15% How often the speed shows a small wobble at rest. 0 means never. 100 means every update.
Vary altitude (m) 1.5 m How much the reported height moves. 0 gives a flat height. Shows only while Vary altitude is on.

GPS realism

Each option copies real GPS behavior, to avoid detection by app checks.

OptionDefaultWhat it does
Hold bearing when stationary On Keeps your last direction when you stop, instead of snapping back to north.
Vary altitude On Moves the reported altitude slightly. The Vary altitude (m) value above sets how much.
Use real-world elevation On Looks up the real ground height at your fake location every minute, instead of a fixed 35 m. Needs internet. Falls back to 35 m if the lookup fails. Pauses while an altitude override is set.
GPS warm-up simulation Off Starts each session with low accuracy that improves over about 30 seconds.
Realistic satellite count On Reports 7 to 14 visible satellites, and 6 to 12 in use.
Simulate signal dropouts Off About every 10 seconds, the signal freezes for about 2 seconds. Most apps show the freeze. Does not run during route playback or walk-to.

OptionDefaultWhat it does

Choose which quick-access features appear in the floating widget and on the map screen. Each feature has one checkbox for the widget and one for the map. Drag the handle to reorder. Both use the same order. Starting and stopping simulation is always available from the top bar.

Capture

Finishes the one-time Capture setup, turns Capture on or off, and sets the browser. It can also restore your usual default browser. You can also pick an app to open after a map link is handled. See Location Links.

Debug stats

Shows coordinates, speed, altitude, accuracy, heading, and update rate in the expanded widget. Off by default.

Language

The app supports English, Simplified Chinese (CN), and Traditional Chinese (TW).

Android versionHow to change
Android 13 and newer Phone Settings → Apps → locationjoystick → Language. The app switches at once.
Android 9 to 12 Settings → Menus, then pick a language from the menu in the top bar. The app restarts.

On Android 9 to 12, overlays and the notification use the new language the next time a session starts.

Want to help translate? See the Contributing guide or ask in the Discord.

Roaming

Defaults for Walk around the block and Planting.

OptionDefaultWhat it does
Walk around the block — Radius 500 m The farthest distance from the start that random stops can land. Maximum 100 km.
Walk around the block — Route distance 1 km The total distance to walk. Allowed range: 50 m to 50 km.
Walk around the block — Speed Walk Starting speed. The roaming sheet offers Walk, Run, and Bike. Settings also offers Slow Walk and Drive.
Follow roads On Walk around the block only. Follows walkable paths. Uses straight lines if the road service is unavailable.
Return to start On Walk around the block only. Walks back to the starting position when the walk ends.
Planting — Starting radius 5 m How close to the center the spiral starts.
Planting — Ending radius 39 m How far out the spiral opens before it tightens again. Maximum 200 m.
Planting — Speed Bike Starting speed: Walk, Run, or Bike. Separate from the Walk around the block speed.
Planting — Infinite loop On Repeats until you stop. Turn it off to set a loop count from 1 to 99 (default 1). One loop is one full expand and tighten.

Once roaming starts, changing speed in the app or widget overrides the starting speed.

External services

The app is offline-first and needs no account. Each service is used only while its feature is active.

ServiceWhen usedWhat is sent
Road routing (OSRM)
router.project-osrm.org		 Routes and roaming that follow roads. The coordinates of your stops or random roaming targets. Nothing that identifies you or your device.
Place search (Nominatim)
nominatim.openstreetmap.org		 Place search in the route creator and favorites map picker, when you type a search and the setting is on. The text you type. No coordinates and nothing that identifies you.
Place search backup (Photon)
photon.komoot.io		 Place search and place-name lookup when the main search cannot be reached or is off. The text you type, or the coordinates of the spot you look up. Nothing that identifies you.
Map images (OpenStreetMap)
tile.openstreetmap.org		 Whenever you move or zoom a map. Only which part of the map to show. No personal data.
