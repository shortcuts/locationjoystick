Settings

Every setting, grouped as in the app. Export, import, and reset are on Backup & Transfer. For permission problems, see Troubleshooting.

Movement & GPS

Speed profiles

Edits apply at once, even during movement. The km/h ↔ mph switch changes every speed in the app. There is no upper limit. Speeds below 0.04 km/h are raised to 0.04 km/h, because zero blocks movement.

Slow Walk

Default: 1.1 km/h

Strolling. Off in the Speed cycle.

Walk

Default: 2.0 km/h

Regular walking pace.

Run

Default: 8.0 km/h

Jogging. Follows footpaths when Follow roads is on.

Bike

Default: 15.0 km/h

Cycling. Follows bike lanes when Follow roads is on.

Drive

Default: 54.0 km/h

Driving. Off in the Speed cycle. Triggers the speed warning.

Walk, Run, and Bike are in the Speed cycle by default. Slow Walk and Drive are not.

Speed warning

Speeds above 28.8 km/h (17.9 mph) show a warning. The warning does not block anything.

Location randomness

Small random changes to each location update, like real GPS drift. Set a value to 0 to turn it off. Wobble and altitude show in feet when you use mph.

Wobble when still

Default: 0.8 m

Largest random shift while you stand still. Maximum 50 m.

Wobble while moving

Default: 0.3 m

Largest random shift while you move. Keep it below the still value. Maximum 50 m.

Largest change per update

Default: 1 m

How far the shift can change in one update. Allowed range: 0.1 to 5 m.

Speed variation when still

Default: 5%

Random percentage added to or taken from the reported speed at rest. 0 looks perfectly still.

Speed variation while moving

Default: 5%

Random percentage added to or taken from the reported speed while you move. Allowed range: 0 to 50%.

Idle wobble frequency

Default: 15%

How often the speed shows a small wobble at rest. 0 means never. 100 means every update.

Altitude variation

Default: 1.5 m

How much the reported height moves. 0 gives a flat height. Shows only while Vary altitude is on.

GPS realism

Each option makes the fake location behave more like a real GPS.

Hold bearing when stationary

Default: On

Keeps your last direction when you stop, instead of snapping back to north.

Vary altitude

Default: On

Moves the reported altitude slightly. The Altitude variation value above sets how much.

Use real-world elevation

Default: On

Looks up the real ground height at your fake location every minute. Needs internet. If the lookup fails, uses 35 m. Pauses while an altitude override is set.

GPS warm-up simulation

Default: Off

Starts each session with low accuracy that improves over about 30 seconds.

Realistic satellite count

Default: On

Reports 7 to 14 visible satellites, and 6 to 12 in use.

Simulate signal dropouts

Default: Off

About every 10 seconds, the signal freezes for about 2 seconds. Most apps show the freeze. Does not run during route playback or walk-to.

Reset elevation override

Default: —

Clears a height set with the altitude override button, so the automatic height resumes. Set the button up under Privacy.

Location memory

Remember last location

Default: On

Restores your last fake position when you reopen the app. Starts from your real location if nothing is saved.

Menus

Appearance

Light mode

Default: Off

Switches the app and widget lists from dark to bright. Overlay buttons stay dark.

Map source

Map source

Default: OpenStreetMap

OpenStreetMap or Amap (高德). Amap is faster in mainland China. The app corrects its coordinate shift automatically.

Place search

At least one service must stay on. If one cannot be reached, the app tries the next one that is on.

Nominatim (OpenStreetMap)

Default: On

The main search service. Turn it off if it is blocked where you are. A service that fails is skipped for 10 minutes.

Photon (Komoot)

Default: On

The backup search service. Works well from mainland China.

Privacy

Hide teleport features

Default: Off

Removes every "jump to location" option: map, favorites, routes, Group Sync, and paste. Routes walk to the first stop instead.

Hide floating widget

Default: Off

Keeps the widget button off the screen while spoofing is on. Other overlays keep working.

Hide notification icon

Default: Off

Removes the "Mock location active" status bar icon. The notification stays in the shade (Android requires it).

Show route jump buttons

Default: Off

Adds "Previous stop" and "Next stop" buttons while a route plays. Hidden when Hide teleport features is on.

Floating map quick walk

Default: Off

A tap on the floating map walks there at once, with no popup. See Overlays.

Show altitude override button

Default: Off

Adds a widget button to type a fixed height. See Overlays.

Capture

Capture setup, the on/off switch, the browser choice, and the app to open after a map link. See Capture for each step.

Debug stats

Shows coordinates, speed, altitude, accuracy, heading, and update rate in the expanded widget. Off by default.

Language

The app supports English, Simplified Chinese (CN), and Traditional Chinese (TW).

Android 13 and newer

Phone Settings → Apps → locationjoystick → Language. The app switches at once.

Android 9 to 12

Settings → Menus, then pick a language from the menu in the top bar. The app restarts.

On Android 9 to 12, overlays and the notification switch language at the next session start.

To help translate, see the Contributing guide.

Roaming

Defaults for Walk around the block and Planting.

Walk around the block — Radius

Default: 500 m

The farthest distance from the start that random stops can land. Maximum 100 km.

Walk around the block — Route distance

Default: 1 km

The total distance to walk. Allowed range: 50 m to 50 km.

Walk around the block — Speed

Default: Walk

Starting speed. The roaming sheet offers Walk, Run, and Bike. Settings also offers Slow Walk and Drive.

Follow roads

Default: On

Walk around the block only. Follows walkable paths. Uses straight lines if the road service is unavailable.

Return to start

Default: On

Walk around the block only. Walks back to the starting position when the walk ends.

Planting — Starting radius

Default: 5 m

How close to the center the spiral starts.

Planting — Ending radius

Default: 39 m

How far out the spiral opens before it tightens again. Maximum 200 m.

Planting — Speed

Default: Bike

Starting speed: Walk, Run, or Bike. Separate from the Walk around the block speed.

Planting — Infinite loop

Default: On

Repeats until you stop. Turn it off to set a loop count from 1 to 99 (default 1). One loop is one full expand and tighten.

Once roaming starts, changing speed in the app or widget overrides the starting speed.

External services

The app is offline-first and needs no account. Each service is used only while its feature is active, except map images, which load whenever a map is shown.

Road routing (OSRM)

router.project-osrm.org

When used: Routes and roaming that follow roads.

What is sent: The coordinates of your stops or random roaming targets. Nothing that identifies you or your device.

Place search (Nominatim)

nominatim.openstreetmap.org

When used: Place search in the route creator and favorites map picker, when you type a search and the setting is on.

What is sent: The text you type. No coordinates and nothing that identifies you.

Place search backup (Photon)

photon.komoot.io

When used: Place search and place-name lookup when the main search cannot be reached or is off.

What is sent: The text you type, or the coordinates of the spot you look up. Nothing that identifies you.

Map images (OpenStreetMap)

tile.openstreetmap.org

When used: Whenever you move or zoom a map.

What is sent: Only which part of the map to show. No personal data.
