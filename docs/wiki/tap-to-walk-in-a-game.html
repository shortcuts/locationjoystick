Tap to walk in a game

Walk toward a spot by tapping the game screen, without opening the app.

Before you start

  • The Display over other apps permission is on.
  • The floating widget is visible.

Steps

  1. Open Settings → Menus → Tap to Walk.
  2. Turn on the screen overlay and tap Agree on the confirmation screen.
  3. Set Scale to match how far your game is zoomed in. Lower is closer.
  4. Switch to your game and expand the widget.
  5. Tap the crosshair button, then tap where you want to walk.

Result

Your fake location walks toward the spot you tapped. The overlay then disappears.

More

Some games detect fake locations and act on your account. Use this only where you accept that risk. If the game map rotates, set up compass orientation. All settings are on the Overlays page.
