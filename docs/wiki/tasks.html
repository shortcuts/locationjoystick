Common tasks

Short guides for one goal each. Pick the one that matches what you want to do.

Teleport to a place

Search for a place and jump there at once with a teleport.

Loop a route

Replay a saved route again and again until you stop it.

Follow a friend

Make a second phone walk to the position of a first phone on the same Wi-Fi.

Move to a new phone

Copy your routes, favorites, and settings to another phone with a QR code.

Tap to walk in a game

Tap your game's screen to walk your fake location toward that spot.

Collect links into a route

Tap map links in other apps to build a list of points, then save the list as a route.

Looking for a specific control? See the Map, Overlays, or Settings pages.
