Teleport to a place

Jump your fake location to any place in the world.

Before you start

  • Spoofing is on. Start it from the Home screen if it is off.
  • Teleport features are not hidden in Settings.

Steps

  1. Open the map and tap the search button (magnifying glass).
  2. Type a place name, address, or landmark. You can also type coordinates.
  3. Pick a result. A marker appears there.
  4. Press and hold the marker, then tap Teleport here.

Result

Your marker jumps to the place. A running route or roam ends first.

More

Games block quick jumps. Check the suggested wait before your next teleport. To walk instead, pick Walk here in step 4.
